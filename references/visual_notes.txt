DearThreado Visual Reference

Overall feeling:
Warm + Creative + Playful + Personal + Heartfelt + Premium-Cute.

The website should feel:
"A beautiful little place where you find something made meaningful
for someone you care about."

Purple remains the primary brand direction.

Use:
- soft lavender-tinted warm white
- controlled lavender/pink/cream supporting tones
- subtle golden accents
- rounded cards
- soft spacing
- expressive headings
- readable body text
- gentle animations
- subtle sparkles
- intentional heart usage

The thread is a major visual identity element.

The thread represents:
connection between thought, creation, person and memory.

Use the supplied screenshots as visual direction.

Do not redesign the visual identity into a generic e-commerce template.
